# Product Claims

Product Claims let participants redeem their points (and, where enabled, cash)
for products and rewards from the program catalogue.

## How to claim a product

1. Open the rewards catalogue.
2. Browse or search for the product you want.
3. Check the points cost shown on the product. Make sure your points balance is
   equal to or greater than the cost.
4. Add the product to your cart and start checkout.
5. Confirm your shipping or delivery details.
6. Submit the claim. The points are deducted from your balance and an order is
   created.

## Tracking and managing a claim

- After a claim is submitted you receive an order confirmation.
- The status moves through stages such as Submitted, Approved, Fulfilled and
  Shipped/Delivered.
- If a claim is rejected or cancelled, the points are returned to your balance.

## Common questions

- **Not enough points?** Earn more through Recognition or Learn & Earn, then
  return to the catalogue.
- **Wrong item or address?** Contact program support before the order is
  fulfilled; changes may not be possible after shipping.
