# Points, Badges and Cash Rewards

The program rewards participants in three ways: points, badges and cash.

## Points

- Points are the main reward currency. You earn them through Recognition and
  Learn & Earn activities.
- Points accumulate in your balance and can be redeemed for products through
  Product Claims.
- Your current points balance is shown on your profile and account summary.

## Badges

- Badges represent achievements, milestones, or recognized behaviours.
- You earn badges through Recognition and by completing certain Learn & Earn
  activities.
- Earned badges appear on your profile. Badges are a form of status and are not
  spendable like points or cash.

## Cash rewards

- Where the program enables cash, recognition can include a cash amount that is
  added to your cash balance.
- Cash rewards are subject to the sender's available budget and program rules.

## Balances

Your account summary shows your points balance, cash balance and the badges you
have earned. If a balance looks wrong, check your recent Recognition and Learn &
Earn activity, or contact program support.
