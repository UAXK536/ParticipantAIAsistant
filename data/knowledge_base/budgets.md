# Budgets

Budgets control how many points or how much cash can be given out as rewards
over a period. They keep recognition and reward spending within program limits.

## Types of budget

- **Recognition budget** — points or cash a manager can award to others during
  a period (for example monthly or quarterly).
- **Program/funding budget** — the overall pool funding rewards for a group,
  team, or the whole program.

## How budgets affect participants

- When you send recognition with points or cash, the amount is deducted from
  your available recognition budget.
- If your budget is exhausted, you can still send badge-only or message-only
  recognition until the budget resets.
- Budgets typically reset at the start of each period; unused amounts may or may
  not carry over depending on program rules.

## Checking your budget

Open the Budgets section to see your available balance, the period it covers,
and how much you have already used. Review it before sending a large reward.
