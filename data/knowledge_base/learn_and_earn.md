# Learn & Earn

Learn & Earn rewards participants for completing learning activities such as
courses, quizzes, and training modules. Completing activities earns points and
can unlock badges.

## How it works

1. Open the Learn & Earn area to see available activities.
2. Each activity shows the points (and any badge) you can earn on completion.
3. Complete the module or pass the quiz to meet the activity's requirements.
4. Points are credited to your balance automatically once the activity is
   marked complete.
5. Some activities award a badge in addition to points when you finish.

## Tips for earning more

- Complete required modules first; they often have the highest point values.
- Watch for time-limited or featured activities with bonus points.
- Revisit the catalogue regularly because new activities are added over time.

## Where the points go

Points earned through Learn & Earn are added to the same balance used for
Product Claims, so you can save them toward a reward in the catalogue.
