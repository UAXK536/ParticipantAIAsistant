# Recognition

Recognition lets participants and managers celebrate great work by sending
points, badges, or cash rewards along with a personal message.

## How to send recognition

1. Open the Recognition area of the program.
2. Choose the person (or team) you want to recognize.
3. Select a recognition type or badge that matches the behaviour you are
   celebrating (for example "Team Player" or "Customer First").
4. Optionally attach reward points or a cash amount, subject to your available
   budget.
5. Add a short, specific message describing what they did and why it mattered.
6. Submit. The recipient is notified and any points or cash are added to their
   balance.

## What participants can earn from recognition

- **Points** — accumulate and can be redeemed in the rewards catalogue.
- **Badges** — visible on the participant's profile and represent milestones
  or behaviours.
- **Cash** — added to the participant's cash balance where the program allows
  cash rewards.

## Recognition budgets

Recognition that includes points or cash draws from a recognition budget.
Managers have a periodic budget; when it is exhausted they can still send
badge-only or message-only recognition. Check the Budgets section to see what
is available before sending a reward.
